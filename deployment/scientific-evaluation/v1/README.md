# AxonAI-v1 prospective scientific evaluation · protocol v1.0

**Status on 2026-11-24:** proposed for review at Git HEAD `a1501ac21bace58304018ca941f735269899bf7f`. The implemented components have CPU-only synthetic exercises. No pilot item, scientific score or performance result has been collected. The first experiment asks how often the final adapter changes the correctness of the same task on a pinned base, and whether the evaluation can measure that difference reliably. A negative or null difference is a valid outcome.

## Design and scope of inference

A is bare Qwen3.8-27B at `1d4bf0f2ff6012fd82039f2fa52739d0dd7c60c0`. B adds the original final adapter to that same base. The private runner uses two exclusive L40S devices for both arms, BF16, the same local software image, exact chat tokens, greedy decoding, attention backend, placement, stopping rules and context budget. Pin the device identities and image with the worker freeze. Use one response per item per arm, fixed attempt policy and blinded scoring. The merged BF16 release on L40S is a subsequent replication condition. The common 384-token completion allowance is a proposed cost/length tradeoff. Test the rubric and length envelope on separate development fixtures before freeze, especially for reasoning and code. Report cap hits per arm and family. A later V100 comparison requires locally validated matched FP16 base and adapted arms on the same V100 configuration. Analyze each hardware/precision comparison separately. Numerical differences from separately applied LoRA, merging or FP16 conversion can change text.

The original SFT contained 10,684 PMC-derived records from 4,230 articles. The original 20,000-row staging artifact and 19,257-row candidate selection were separate steps. The final frozen bytes have SHA-256 `641b43e32f3d69fa6fbf74754772e976777a26383c27de2692b90670c5dc5d07`. The training configuration used rank-16 LoRA, alpha 32, BF16 and two epochs. The result records 668 optimizer steps and no validation-set binding. The BF16 and FP16 releases passed engineering validation. These facts motivate screening by article and derivative family and keeping engineering checks separate from scientific endpoints. ## Pilot sampling frame

The allocation retains the proposed 80 pairs. It dedicates 48 pairs to biomedical knowledge, reasoning and evidence behavior while reserving explicit slots for methods, abstention, retention and transfer. Each item has one primary family; cross-cutting tags in private workpapers do not add observations. These quotas define a diagnostic mixture. They do not estimate the prevalence of tasks in biomedical practice.

| Family in registry | Pairs | Construction and main assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| biomedical_factual | 12 | Sample across subfields. Freeze authoritative, date-bounded references, distractor rationale and a unique target. |
| biomedical_reasoning | 12 | Require at least two independently checkable inferences. Annotate indispensable premises, conclusion and material errors for two experts. |
| evidence_qa | 12 | Supply rights-cleared passages and local evidence IDs. Include close distractors. Score the answer and claim-to-passage support. |
| hallucination_citations | 12 | Include supported, unanswerable and incorrect-premise demands, with supplied evidence and explicit citation scope. Record unsupported assertions, incorrect attribution and absence of required citations separately. |
| experimental_design | 6 | Ask about benign study designs. Lock hypothesis, controls, confounding, randomization, measurement and analysis criteria. Require two methods reviewers. |
| statistical_reasoning | 6 | Use curated or internally constructed summaries. Lock estimand, units, acceptable rounding and interpretations. Dual review for open-ended explanations. |
| scientific_programming | 6 | Pose bounded scientific computation over synthetic inputs. Independently check reference solutions, hidden tests, edge cases and numeric tolerances in the approved executor. |
| uncertainty_abstention | 4 | Balance two answerable and two insufficient-evidence cases from distinct source families. Score both unnecessary abstention and unjustified answering. |
| general_retention | 5 | Sample non-biomedical logic, instruction following and quantitative reasoning, using verifiable targets. |
| scientific_transfer | 5 | Sample non-biomedical scientific disciplines. Require external-discipline expertise or an unambiguous objective key. |

1. An independent item lead defines a dated source frame, candidate sources, languages (English text only for this pilot), subfields, difficulties, evidence availability, author assignments and scoring forms before writing. Use rights-cleared, non-sensitive material and self-contained sources. Record which properties were sampled and which were purposively balanced. Use separate human authors and independent solvers, neither selecting examples from model outputs. Do not ask either evaluated model to author or validate items. Track related experiments, datasets, articles, translations, template variants, author conflicts and prior access. Use synthetic benign data for programming.
2. The lead prepares excess candidates within each family, including reserves. Keep difficulty and answerability targets in the frame. In the abstention family admit one ranked subgroup per answerability class and take two each, as described below. Lock exact text, permitted context, answer contract, private key/rubric/tests and version. A second qualified person solves from the prompt alone and checks the locked key against the cited source; resolve ambiguity and rescreen edits before freeze. Record review time. Reject any construction that requires live web search by a model.
3. Rights clearance applies to source text, excerpts, prompts, keys, viewing by reviewers, retention and any later redistribution. Record stable source IDs, versions, licensor, license/permission, attribution obligations and permitted uses in a private rights receipt. Public accessibility or historical SFT clearance alone supplies no evaluation permission. The custodian checks known authorship, overlapping families and exposure. Use the private registry schema, signed clearance/contamination receipts, and one canonical source-family component per candidate. For multi-source tasks, group linked source components across the entire frame before the draw. The frame audit resolves shared components before selection.
4. Independently screen all candidates, including proposed reserves. For within-pool sibling items retain at most one, chosen by a precommitted random order set before scoring; rescreen the reduced pool. Keep raw flags and the reason for every decision. `pilot.draw` admits only cleared, independently verified, previously unexposed items with a documented negative disposition. Missing accessible-corpus coverage or an unresolved item-level signal keeps the candidate out of the roster. Record sealed-history coverage separately. The independent custodian may approve a scope-limited diagnostic pilot on documented independently sourced items if sealed overlap remains unknown. Retain that uncertainty in the registry and report, and never convert it into a clean holdout claim. If a family is short, extend authorship, screen again and register a new frame revision. [Private screening](screening.md) · [Item schema](item.schema.json) · [Outcome schema and failure codes](outcome.schema.json)
5. Lock the canonical eligible/ineligible frame digest, content commitments, quota table and selection algorithm. The independent custodian generates a 256-bit draw seed *after* lock, signs the seed receipt and runs `pilot.draw` exactly once. Within family, sort HMAC-SHA256(seed, domain + opaque ID); record selected and reserve ranks. The draw takes the exact quotas from the eligible frame. The uncertainty family takes the two lowest ranks in each answerability class and retains same-class reserves. Reconcile strata and expected source-family distinctness before releasing prompts. The item list, seed, frame and commitments stay private. Archive all versions and exclusions.
6. Preflight rendered lengths on the approved tokenizer, expected task format and feasibility of scoring, with both arms still unobserved. A failed precheck may use the next locked reserve from the same stratum. Record which slot and why, rescreen any changed content, re-freeze the complete 80, then freeze the run ID. After the first model output, retain the denominator of 80 and all failures. Any revised items, rubrics, prompts, budgets or inference settings belong to a new diagnostic run.
7. The historical 51 are excluded wholesale. Keep any prior exposure or development/engineering example in the diagnostic ledger. Freeze pilot artifacts in the exclusion registry before any later study. A future confirmatory study needs independent authorship, disjoint sources/derivatives, a new custody domain, a predeclared target population, sampling design, meaningful effect, endpoint, error budget, analysis, and a power calculation. Authorize its construction separately after this review. [Stage 0 receipt](stage0.md)

## Feasibility acceptance criteria

These are proposed progression thresholds, subject to sign-off **before** the first output. No gate depends on the sign of the model difference. They implement the general principle of predeclaring pilot feasibility outcomes and progression criteria. [Eldridge et al., pilot and feasibility reporting](https://www.bmj.com/content/355/bmj.i5239)

| Check | Progression criterion | Disposition if missed |
|---|---|---|
| Sampling and custody | 80 locked eligible items in assigned strata, one independent source-family component per item, all rights/screen/author receipts, zero unexplained access incidents | Hold release. Quarantine affected content and mark exposure in perpetuity. |
| Input and model identity | All 160 scheduled outcomes reconciled; all generated responses have authenticated model, runtime, input and budget parity | Stop interpretation for any integrity mismatch. A documented lost worker remains a failure. |
| Completion and ceilings | At least 76/80 pairs have final scores, at least 80% of each family complete, and at most 4/80 length-limited outputs per arm | Report missing-score bounds, family shortfalls and clipping; revise the next diagnostic protocol. |
| Scoring | All scheduled private keys/rubrics and program suites independently checked before use; deterministic scorers repeat exactly; every item has a terminal outcome or documented pending reason | Repair or version failing components; never impute a pass from a missing score. |
| Human reliability | Two independent ratings for every response assigned expert review, separate adjudication of every disagreement; target ≥80% exact binary pass/fail agreement and linear-weighted ordinal kappa ≥0.60 | Report matrix, marginal imbalance, raw agreement, kappa and sample size. Undefined kappa or sparse categories trigger rubric review and a separate calibration round. |
| Operational readiness | Complete time, token and memory receipts for all generated outputs, load/retry costs accounted for, proposed wall budget met or revised from observed rates and approved | Reforecast before replication. Retain both arms' full response-length distribution. |
| Confirmatory readiness | A statistician can derive a feasible independent sample size from prespecified meaningful differences, conservative discordance and missingness scenarios; custody and rights leads sign off on a separate future study | Keep results descriptive and record the unmet conditions. |

Report every gate even when another fails. Show paired B-minus-A pass rate, wins/losses and both kinds of ties, conservative intervals and missingness bounds overall and by family. Present biomedical-family results alongside retention, transfer and safety-relevant error classes. Report reviewer agreement before adjudication. Keep conciseness, latency and throughput on separate scales. The pilot is neither powered nor sampled to settle scientific superiority.

## Resources and review decision

Reserve two L40S GPUs for four wall hours (eight GPU-hours) provisionally. The estimate, including its Stage 0 rate assumptions and full-cap scenario, is in the inference protocol. At minimum the two expert-reviewed biomedical/method families and the two evidence/citation families comprise 42 items. If both arms deliver responses, that is 84 responses and 168 independent ratings. At an assumed 10–15 minutes per rating, budget 28–42 reviewer-hours before adjudication, other referrals, calibration or authoring. Record actual time by task and status. Reforecast both GPU and human costs from pilot measurements. Use the discordance upper bound and a range of scientifically meaningful effects for confirmatory planning.
